At the Gartner Security & Risk Management Summit 2026, Mark Wojtasiak identified five emerging cybersecurity trends: AI is accelerating both attacks and defenses, identity and nonhuman entities are becoming the primary security control plane, continuous threat exposure management is replacing traditional vulnerability management, the SOC is evolving into a human-AI hybrid workforce, and cyber resilience is becoming the primary executive objective.
